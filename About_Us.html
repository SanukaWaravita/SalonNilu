Welcome to Salon Nilu

Welcome to Nilu Salon, your trusted unisex salon where beauty meets style and confidence. At Nilu, we believe that self-care is for everyone, which is why we offer a wide range of professional services for both men and women. From precision haircuts, styling, coloring, and grooming to rejuvenating skin treatments, we ensure every client feels refreshed and confident. We also specialize in bridal makeovers, offering personalized packages to make your special day truly unforgettable. With a team of skilled stylists and beauty experts, we combine creativity, passion, and the latest trends to deliver services tailored just for you. Whether you’re preparing for a big occasion or simply need a fresh look, Nilu Salon is here to bring out the best version of you in a warm and welcoming atmosphere.
